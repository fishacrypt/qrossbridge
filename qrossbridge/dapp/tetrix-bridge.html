LIVE TESTNET INFRASTRUCTURE

Transfers, made transparent.

1

You send

Deposit QMS on QMS Testnet, or burn wQMS on another network. One transaction from your wallet.

2

The relayer delivers

A relayer watches for your transfer, waits for a few block confirmations, then submits the delivery on the destination chain.

3

You receive

The destination contract marks the transfer as processed exactly once. QROS only shows a transfer as complete after that happens.
